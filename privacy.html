Pixel Clips Poster: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Pixel Clips Poster ("the app") is an internal tool run by Pixel Pixel Company LLC that uploads our own video clips to our own TikTok account, @pixelclippy.

What we collect. When our TikTok account is connected, TikTok gives the app an access token, a refresh token, the account's open ID and its basic profile info (display name and avatar). We don't collect information about any other TikTok users.

How we use it. Only to upload videos to the connected account and check their upload status.

Where it's stored. The tokens are stored in a file on our own computer, readable only by our account. We don't sell or share them with anyone, and we don't use them for ads or analytics.

How long we keep it. Until the account is disconnected. You can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings > Security > Apps and services, and we delete the stored tokens when the account is disconnected.

Changes. We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
